# Episode 1 — "Two Skies, One Grave"

**Runtime:** 13:59 as produced (premiere; target ~15 min)
**Part One:** *The Bruised Sky*
**Purpose:** Hook the audience with a protagonist who appears to be the villain, establish the rules of the gift, and end on a nine-day countdown.

The episode is a **bookend**. It opens and closes on the same rooftop at the same moment. The first time, we watch a villain. The second time, we're standing invisible beside him and realize **the villain is the hero's own face**. He knew we were there, and he was talking to us all along.

## Beat Sheet

### ▌FLASH-FORWARD (0:00–0:13)
Total black, with subliminal two-frame flashes of a sky full of colliding Earths.
- **SEREN** *(O.S., desperate, echoing)*: "Elias, STOP! You'll kill everyone!"
- Silence.
- **ELIAS** *(O.S., calm, close)*: "...Yes."
- One clock tick.

*This is Episode 22, heard without context. It makes our protagonist sound like a world-killer before we've even met him.*

### ▌COLD OPEN — "The Execution" (0:13–3:35)
- Super: **SOMEWHERE ELSE. / YESTERDAY.** A countdown HUD appears: `IMPACT 03:41`.
- **Wide:** Merridale in ruins under an upside-down Earth that fills half the sky. Rain falls *upward*. Buildings peel off the ground. St. Ansel's Clocktower is cracked and frozen at 11:58.
- **The rooftop:** a coat-whipped silhouette, **MISTER YESTERDAY**, holds a kneeling woman by the collar. We never see his face, only two pale cyan clock-ring eyes.
- He soothes her like a child ("Shhh. Don't look up."), then explodes ("HAVE TO?!"), laughs, and apologizes ("I get loud near the end. Everyone does.").
- He counts: **"Four thousand. One hundred. And twelve."** The number slams onto the screen and cracks.
- She spits blood: **"The Keepers will find you, Mister Yesterday."** It's the first time we hear his name. He laughs: "The Keepers are knitting a sweater for a corpse."
- He tears light out of her chest and into his fist.
- At `01:00` he stops, tilts his head, and **turns to the camera**: "Oh. And you. Yes. *You.* Standing there in the dark, pretending you're not there. I know that trick. I invented it."
- "Watch closely. This is what you become."
- The light hardens into a **black obsidian key**, which cracks.
- "Hate me. Good. Hate's a better teacher than fear."
- Her eyes slide past him, to us: **"...Elias..."**
- The Earths touch. White. `00:00`. **Smash to black.**

### ▌TITLE (3:35–3:52)
Violet threads weave the obsidian key in the dark, and it shatters into **THE ANCHOR OF YESTERDAY**.

### ▌ACT ONE — "The Archivist" (3:52–7:16)
- **Match cut** to the same wide of Merridale, intact at dawn, with birds. Super: **MERRIDALE. TODAY.** The countdown HUD flickers once, a ghost, and disappears.
- **Thorne Archival.** Dust in light shafts. Elias, gloved, catalogues.
- **V.O.:** *"Everything that was ever made remembers being made..."* His gift is explained in his own words. "So I don't touch much."
- **The Rules** stamp onto the screen as kinetic cards, ending with *"Four. Don't open the letter."*
- **Voicemail** from Dr. Hale's office: the scans, the lesions, *"how much time—"*. Elias deletes it and slides a paperweight onto the unopened hospital envelope.
- **Mrs. Pemberton** arrives with her late husband's stopped pocket watch. "I just want to know if he was happy. When he bought it."
- Elias breaks a rule out of kindness: "...Three minutes." He removes a glove.
- **Retrospection #1 (gentle):** the shop unspools into gold threads, and we're in **1962**, in a watchmaker's workshop. Young Henry paces, terrified and beaming. The engraving reads *"For Odile — every second, yours."* The watchmaker says "Good. Means it matters."
- **Snap back.** A nosebleed. "He was terrified. And he was the happiest man I've ever seen." She weeps and smiles: "Every second. He used to say that."
- Alone, he looks at the letter. **V.O.:** *"Everything remembers being made. I've just never wanted to know how anything ends."*

### ▌ACT TWO — "The Bruised Sky" (7:16–8:50)
- The radio dissolves into static. The grandfather clock's pendulum **stops mid-swing, at an angle**. Dust freezes. A drop of tea floats **upward**.
- Outside, the sky **bleeds purple from the zenith, like ink in water**. Starlings hang motionless. A child asks, "Mum... why is the sky bleeding?"
- For one instant, a **ghost of an inverted skyline** hangs above them: the same clocktower, upside down.
- Elias's bare hand brushes a lamppost, and **every object in the street remembers at once**. A storm of kinetic text: *CAST IRON, 1891 · SHE WAITED HERE EVERY TUESDAY · FIRST KISS, 1974 · HE NEVER CAME BACK*. It lasts nine seconds.
- The sky snaps back to blue, and people laugh nervously.
- **News:** "an atmospheric glitch." **Dr. Marlowe:** "Gravity didn't fail. It... doubled. As though the atmosphere was *rejecting* something."
- **V.O.:** *"Nine seconds. I told myself it was nothing. I'm very good at that."*

### ▌ACT THREE — "The Woman with the Key" (8:50–10:37)
- Night and rain. Elias flips the sign to CLOSED.
- **BANG.** The door flies open and the bell shrieks. **SEREN** staggers in, bleeding, clutching a bundle that glows violet through the cloth. "Elias Thorne. The man who touches things."
- She unwraps a **shattered obsidian key**, smoking and pulsing. Every object in the shop **leans toward it**, and his gloves smoke.
- "Where did you get that?" / **"From a world that died yesterday."**
- "I catalogue things. I'm not—" / "Brave? I know. I'm not asking you to be brave. I'm asking you to touch it."
- **A seam splits the air.** A glitching black hand, **a Fray**, claws through. Seren **stitches the air shut with Aether**, violet-silver threads from her fingertips, like a surgeon.
- "It's warm. Rule three." / **"In nine days, everything you love will be cold."**
- He won't move, so **she grabs his wrist, strips his glove, and presses the key into his palm.**
- *Seed:* as he's ripped away, her face shows calculation, not relief. A breath: **"Good."**

### ▌ACT FOUR — "The Incursion" (10:37–12:17)
- **Retrospection #2 (violent):** the shop **shatters like glass**. Elias is hurled down a tunnel of violet threads past thousands of Earths. His gold ring timer **cracks and turns violet**.
- **He lands on the rooftop from the cold open, behind Mister Yesterday.** `IMPACT 01:08`.
- He screams and no one hears. He grabs Mister Yesterday's arm and his hand passes through.
- "...somewhere it will finally matter." Mister Yesterday stops, tilts his head, and turns. The light of the colliding worlds sweeps across his face. **IT'S ELIAS.** He's older, gaunt, scarred through the left eye. **Split-frame:** our Elias's face | Mister Yesterday's face.
- Now the cold open's lines land with their real target: "Oh. And you... I know that trick. I invented it."
- **New line we didn't hear in the cold open:** "Hello, Elias."
- "Watch closely. This is what you become." The key forms. "Hate me. Good."
- **Another new line:** "And when she comes for you — and she will —" and **the roar swallows the rest**.
- The dying woman's eyes find our Elias. **She can see him.**
  **"Elias... you aren't trying to stop the incursions. You are causing them."**
- *Blink-and-miss (6 frames):* a masked figure on a floating shard high above, watching.
- The worlds touch. White.

### ▌TAG — "Nine Days" (12:17–13:42)
- **Snap back.** Blood from his nose and ear. The key cools on the floorboards.
- Seren holds his face: "Elias. What did you see?" / **"Me. I saw me."**
- "Not me. *A* me. Then you understand why I came."
- She raises the blind. The night sky is faintly bruised, and on the horizon, **enormous and pale, is the curve of another Earth**, like a moon too big for the sky.
- **"Seren. Keeper of the Loom. And that is the world that's going to kill yours."** / "How long?" / **"Nine days."**
- **V.O.:** *"Rule three. Never touch anything that's still warm. I should have had a rule about people."*
- HUD: `INCURSION — 08D 23H 59M 59S`. One second ticks. **Black.**

### ▌STINGER — "Atropos" (13:42–13:59)
- The Loom: a void strung with thousands of luminous threads. **A porcelain mask with a golden kintsugi crack.** She runs a finger along the threads, and one flares gold.
- **ATROPOS** *(layered)*: "There you are."
- Silver scissors cut the neighboring thread. Far away, a world winks out.
- *(Almost human)*: "...I'm sorry."

## Double-Meaning Table — Mister Yesterday's Lines

Every line works twice. The first reading is what the audience believes in Ep 1; the second is what they realize in Ep 20.

| Line | First watch (villain) | Rewatch (hero) |
|---|---|---|
| "Shhh. Don't look up." | Taunting his prey | Sparing a dying woman the sight of her own sky |
| "Two skies. One grave." | Gloating | Grief. He's buried thousands of worlds. |
| "The first time I did this, I was so scared I couldn't hold the knife." | A killer reminiscing | The first Seren he had to free. He's never stopped being scared. |
| "HAVE TO?!" | Psychotic rage | The fury of a man with no other choice |
| "I get loud near the end. Everyone does." | Black humor | He's watched 4,112 endings and knows how people break. |
| "4,112… what every single one had in common? **Me.**" | He destroyed them all | Every world that died had an Elias in it. The immune response hunts Thornes. He's confessing to *existing*. |
| "The Keepers are knitting a sweater for a corpse." | Contempt | An accurate diagnosis. The Keepers' methods can't work. |
| "I'm taking it somewhere it will finally matter." | Absorbing her vitality | Forging the Anchor key that will save every world |
| "I know that trick. I invented it." | Creepy omniscience | Literally true. He's Elias, and he has the same gift. |
| "This is what you become." | A prophecy of villainy | A warning (*don't run from death*), and also the truth: you'll become the man who sacrifices. |
| "Hate me. Good. Hate's a better teacher than fear." | Sadism | The whole plan. Elias has to hate Mister Yesterday more than he fears dying. |
| "And when she comes for you —" | A threat | A warning about Seren, cut off before it could matter |

**Alt-Seren's whisper** is also revealed in stages:
- Ep 1 cold open: *"...Elias..."*
- Ep 1 Act 4: *"Elias... you aren't trying to stop the incursions. You are causing them."*
- Ep 20 (full): *"...You are causing them. **So only you can end them.**"*
