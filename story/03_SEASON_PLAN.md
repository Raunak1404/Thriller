# THE ANCHOR OF YESTERDAY — Season Plan

**Structure:** 3 Parts × 8 episodes = 24 episodes.
**Runtimes:** ~10 min per episode. Ep 1 and each Part finale (Ep 8, 16, 24) run ~15 min.

| Part | Title | Episodes | Engine | Ends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One** | *The Bruised Sky* | 1–8 | A **nine-day countdown** to Merridale's Incursion | The Curator dies saving the world he seemed to be destroying |
| **Two** | *The Loom* | 9–16 | Hunted by the **masked Weaver** through the Keepers' world and the Parliament of Arthurs | **Twist 1:** the Weaver is Lyra from the future |
| **Three** | *The Anchor* | 17–24 | The truth, the betrayal, the sacrifice | A normal afternoon, and a singular universe |

---

## How the "Arthur Is the Villain" Illusion Breaks Down

The audience's belief about Arthur goes through stages, and each one feels like the final answer until the next.

| Stage | Episodes | The audience believes… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ep 1 | **Arthur is the villain.** An older Arthur executes Lyras and destroys worlds. "This is what you become." |
| 2 | Ep 2–7 | Arthur is fighting *against* becoming the villain. The Curator hunts them and kills Lyras, and the Archive of Ends is a museum of his murders. |
| 3 | Ep 7 | **Arthur is the cause anyway.** Incursions only happen where he and Lyra go. The whisper was right. |
| 4 | Ep 8 | *First crack.* The "villain" dies holding two Earths apart to save Merridale. Why would a monster do that? |
| 5 | Ep 17 | **Doubled down.** The original Arthur fractured reality out of fear of death. Every Arthur is a parasite. |
| 6 | Ep 20 | **The truth.** The Curator was a hero who played a monster on purpose. Every "execution" was mercy and preparation. |
| 7 | Ep 22–24 | **Arthur is the hero.** He chooses the one thing Prime Arthur couldn't: his own ending. |

# PART ONE — *THE BRUISED SKY*

**Spine:** Nine days until Merridale collides with another Earth. Each episode is a day closer, and the HUD counts it down.
**Arthur's arc:** Coward → student → a man who stands his ground.

### Ep 1 — "Two Skies, One Grave" *(15 min · Day 9)*
See `02_EPISODE_01_STORY.md`. The Execution, the archivist, the bruised sky, the key, and the reveal that the monster wears Arthur's face. *"You are causing them."* Nine days.

### Ep 2 — "Nine Days" *(Day 8)*
Lyra explains the Loom, Aether, Keepers, and Incursions in a dazzling **motion-graphics "Loom" sequence**: universes as threads, two threads drifting together, a collision. *"When a world dies, its neighbors bruise."* Arthur refuses to get involved and tries to go back to his rules. A seam opens in the market and **Frays** pour through. Lyra fights alone, and Arthur hides behind a stall. A child is nearly taken, and Arthur, terrified, pulls her out. It's his first choice. He agrees to train.
**Closing image:** the Curator on the clocktower ledge, watching. A hand-written note pinned to the shop door: *"8."*

### Ep 3 — "Aether" *(Days 7–6)*
A training montage with the series' best kinetic-type sequence: Aether sigils as glowing glyphs, and Arthur failing over and over. **Discovery:** his retrospection lets him see the "crafting moment" of a seam, which means he can **step through it**. His first accidental jump drops him into an empty reality for 40 terrifying seconds. Banter, burns, and a growing bond with Lyra.
**Climax:** Arthur stabilizes his first micro-incursion, a bubble where a street from another world overlaps his own. The Curator steps out of the bubble: *"You learn fast. I did too."* He overpowers Arthur without effort and takes nothing. Leaving, he says: *"Ask her where she was the day the sky bled."*

### Ep 4 — "Brass and Root" *(Day 5)*
**First world-jump:** a **steampunk metropolis, Ironhollow**, is days from colliding with a **jungle Earth, Verdance**. Gearwork towers are threaded with vines. They meet **Brass Arthur**, a celebrated inventor and his world's beloved hero, who is everything our Arthur isn't. The Incursion triggers **hours after they arrive**. They save a train full of children, but the worlds still collide. Brass Arthur dies covering their escape. *"Funny. I always thought I'd be braver than you."*
**Seed:** the timing. Lyra brushes it off.

### Ep 5 — "Gravity Is a Spell" *(Day 4)*
**A world where magic replaced gravity** (Levant). Cities float on choral spells and people sing to keep the ground down. It's crashing into a **dead cosmos** (Ashfield), a black, silent Earth. This is the visual centerpiece of Part 1. The Curator appears, and in front of Arthur he **"executes" Levant's Lyra**. For the first time Arthur **fights** him, badly but bravely. The Curator lets him land a blow, then disappears. *"You're getting braver. That's going to be a problem."*
**Seed:** the Curator looked at *our* Lyra, not Arthur, when he said it.

### Ep 6 — "The Archive of Ends" *(Day 3)*
Arthur tracks the Curator by touching the objects he leaves behind and finds his lair: **the Archive of Ends**, a cathedral-sized pocket dimension with **4,112 objects from 4,112 dead worlds**, each labeled in *Arthur's own handwriting*. One wall holds the names of every Lyra. Arthur breaks down, sure he's destined to become a serial killer. Lyra: *"Then don't."* They come close to a kiss. Arthur finds one object labeled **"FOR HIM"** and touches it. He glimpses the Curator **weeping**, but Lyra pulls him out of the vision *too fast*.
**Seed:** what didn't Lyra want him to see?

### Ep 7 — "The Pattern" *(Days 2–1)*
The archivist becomes a detective. Arthur maps every Incursion they've witnessed onto a wall of string and photographs, a **thriller-montage** episode. The finding is undeniable: Incursions trigger **only after he and Lyra arrive**. The Curator is never first. Paranoia and self-loathing set in: *"She was right. I'm causing them."* Lyra offers a plausible theory: the Curator is tracking them and *using* them. Arthur sets a trap for the Curator, using himself as bait. They return to Merridale. The second Earth now **fills half the sky**, and the city is evacuating.
**Closing:** the HUD reads `00D 23H`.

### Ep 8 — "The Man Who Wears My Face" *(15 min · Day 0 · Part finale)*
Merridale's Incursion begins: rain falls up, the clocktower cracks, and the cold-open imagery becomes *our* world. Arthur confronts the Curator in **St. Ansel's Clocktower**. **The fight is a retrospection duel.** Both Arthurs dive through the crafting moments of everything in the tower, from 1704 bell-casting to a 1940s blackout to the day the clock was wound for the first time, fighting through *the past*. Arthur, brave at last, wins.
Then the Curator does something no villain would. He **throws himself into the friction band between the two Earths** and holds them apart with Aether. The immune response takes **his** Arthur-anchor instead of Merridale's, and the second Earth **recedes**. Dying, he presses the **other half of the obsidian key** into Arthur's hand:
**"I was never chasing you. I was running ahead of *her*."**
He looks up, and Arthur follows his gaze to a **masked figure** standing in the sky.
Lyra tells Arthur the Curator was trying to steal the collision's power and it overloaded him. Arthur wants to believe her. The two halves of the key **fuse**, and a map unfolds in light pointing to **the Incursion Nexus**.
**Stinger:** the Weaver makes a tally mark on her Loom. *"One fewer."*

# PART TWO — *THE LOOM*

**Spine:** Find the masked Weaver who is triggering the Incursions.
**Arthur's arc:** A capable hero who believes he's beaten his dark destiny, until he meets dozens more of himself.

### Ep 9 — "What Is Woven, We Keep"
The key opens a path to **the Selvedge**, the Keepers' sanctum at the hem of reality, where looms the size of mountains weave visible universes. **Elder Keeper Oduya** and the Order put Arthur on trial: his soul frequency matches the "Parasite" of their oldest prophecy, *the Man Who Is Many*. Lyra vouches for him. When Arthur says the Curator is dead, the Keepers celebrate. Oduya alone is silent. He knew him.

### Ep 10 — "Selvedge"
**The Weaver attacks the sanctum** and pulls threads with her bare hands, so worlds collide on command. It's the first proof that Incursions are *being triggered*. A siege episode. Oduya dies shielding Arthur and whispers: *"She holds the scissors. She is not the one holding the needle."* Lyra grieves, and it's unclear whether the grief is real.

### Ep 11 — "The Parliament of Arthurs"
Hiding in a far-flung world, they find **the Parliament**: dozens of surviving Arthurs who've banded together against the cull. **Saint Arthur** (who believes they're all damned), **Grief Arthur**, **Artemis Vance**, and **Little Arthur** (age ten) are among them. They call our Arthur **"the Root,"** the thread closest to the original. Half of them want to protect him and half want to sacrifice him. Political thriller. The Weaver strikes the Parliament's world **because Arthur came**. The pattern again.

### Ep 12 — "Grief Engine"
A world where an Arthur built a machine to resurrect his dead Lyra, which shows Arthur's darkest potential. Our Lyra sees how much every Arthur loves her, and it shakes her. A quiet scene: *"If you had to choose between me and every world?"* / *"I'd choose wrong. I always choose wrong."*

### Ep 13 — "Retrospection in Reverse"
Trapped in a collapsing world, Arthur discovers he can **push the present into an object's past**, rewriting its memory and therefore its present. He uses it to save the Parliament's survivors. Lyra watches him with real fear. This power threatens her plan.

### Ep 14 — "Cut Threads"
Lyra disappears for an hour. Arthur reaches for her loom-sigil to see its crafting moment, and she **stops him violently**. It's their first real fight, and they reconcile carefully. The surviving Keepers reveal that some of the Order has **served the Weaver for centuries**.

### Ep 15 — "The Gate That Knew My Name"
**The Nexus Gate** is sealed to Arthur's original soul frequency. Arthur touches it and sees its crafting moment: **Prime Arthur**, centuries ago, building the gate with godlike Aether. *"No one will ever reach the center. Not even me."* The gate opens for our Arthur. Lyra smiles with relief, for the wrong reasons.

### Ep 16 — "Unmasked" *(15 min · Part finale)*
The Incursion Nexus is a void where infinite Earths hang in slow-motion collision. The battle with the Weaver is the series' biggest action set piece. Arthur breaks her mask.
**TWIST 1: it's Lyra.** She's older, sorrowful, and ancient-eyed, and she looks at young Lyra, not at Arthur.

# PART THREE — *THE ANCHOR*

**Spine:** The truth, then the betrayal, then the sacrifice.
**Arthur's arc:** From the original sinner to the man who chooses his own ending.

### Ep 17 — "Immune Response"
**TWIST 2.** Arthur touches the Weaver's broken mask, which was crafted in the Prime Universe, and the retrospection shows **Prime Arthur** dying, terrified, using Aether to achieve omniscience and **fracture the Prime Universe** to escape death. His soul anchors across every branch like a parasite. **Incursions are the cosmos's immune response**, crushing timelines together to bleed out duplicate Arthurs until one remains. Future Lyra has spent millennia triggering *controlled* collisions to finish the job before reality's framework fails. This is Arthur's lowest point: he *is* the disease.

### Ep 18 — "Two Lyras"
A three-way standoff and the most charged dialogue of the series.
Young Lyra: *"I won't become you."* / Future Lyra: *"You already have. You just haven't noticed yet."*
Arthur refuses both "let the cull finish" and "die now," because he's still afraid. He decides there has to be another way.

### Ep 19 — "The Seal"
**TWIST 3.** Young Lyra steps back and seals Arthur in an Aether cage. She knew everything from the start. She used his retrospection to bypass the Nexus locks, which were tuned to his soul, and the "lessons" were how she got him here. She seals her future self too. Her plan is **the final, absolute Incursion**, crashing every remaining reality into a single point in one night instead of millennia. *"I've seen what I become. I'd rather end the world than be her."*
Callback: as the seal closes, she whispers **"Good."**

### Ep 20 — "What the Curator Knew"
Sealed and powerless, Arthur has one thing left: **the obsidian key**. He touches it a third time, and we see **The Execution a third time.** Now we see everything:
- The Lyra of that world was a beacon for the Weaver, and she **asked** the Curator to free her.
- The Curator was **weeping** behind his cold voice.
- The key was never a Nexus key. It's **the Anchor of Yesterday**, a fixed point Arthur can pull all of reality back through.
- The full whisper: *"You are causing them. **So only you can end them.**"*
- The Curator speaks to the observer directly, and this time it's tender: *"I'm sorry I had to be your monster. You needed someone to hate more than you feared dying."*

**The protagonist was never the villain.** The villain was fear.

### Ep 21 — "Every Earth at Once"
Young Lyra starts the final Incursion, and **every Earth in the multiverse converges** in one sky, the series' visual apex. Across the Loom, the Parliament of Arthurs feel it, and one by one they **let go**, lending their anchors to the Root. Future Lyra, freed, fights her younger self.

### Ep 22 — "The Anchor of Yesterday"
The seal is keyed to Arthur's will to live. **He breaks it by accepting death.** He begins **reverse retrospection**: instead of falling into an object's past, he pulls the whole multiverse back through his own memory. His life plays backwards around them: the shop, Mrs. Pemberton, the letter, his childhood.
Then the flash-forward from Ep 1, now in full context:
**Lyra:** *"Arthur, STOP! You'll kill everyone!"*
**Arthur:** *"...Yes."* (beat) *"Everyone who shouldn't exist. Starting with me."*

### Ep 23 — "Implosion"
He surrenders his existence across every colliding Earth, and every parasitic anchor lets go. The final Incursion **implodes inward**, stitching infinite threads back into one. Farewells: Future Lyra's mask finally falls away, and she smiles: *"Thank you."* Young Lyra understands too late. He takes her hand as everything folds.

### Ep 24 — "A Normal Afternoon" *(15 min · Series finale)*
Arthur wakes in his dusty archival shop on a quiet afternoon. There are no Incursions, no bleeding skies, no Aether. **St. Ansel's chimes noon.** He opens the hospital letter, reads it, and sets it down with peace. We never learn what it says.
The bell chimes. A woman walks in carrying a **shattered obsidian key** and asks if he can repair it. It's **Lyra**, ordinary and human, with no memory of any of it. As she waits, she absently **hums four notes**.
Arthur feels the pull of retrospection in the key and **chooses not to look**. He mends it with gold, kintsugi style, the cracks still showing.
**Lyra:** *"How long will it last?"*
**Arthur** *(warm, quiet smile)*: *"Every second."*
Final shot: the sky. One sky.

## Production Notes for Later Episodes

- **Reuse the Episode 1 asset library.** The rooftop, the skyline, the two-Earth sky, the shop, and the key will appear again in Ep 8, 16, 20, and 22. The engine is data-driven (`episodes/epXX.js`), so a new episode is mostly a new script file plus a few new scene types.
- **New scene types needed per Part:**
  - Part 1: market street with Frays, training void, Ironhollow/Verdance, Levant/Ashfield, Archive of Ends, clocktower interior.
  - Part 2: Selvedge looms, Parliament chamber, Nexus Gate, Nexus void.
  - Part 3: Prime Universe (pure white-gold), multi-Earth convergence, reverse-life montage, the restored shop at noon.
- **Ep 20 replay:** the engine's deterministic renderer makes "same scene, new angle" cheap. Re-render the Execution with a `perspective: 'truth'` parameter (Curator's face lit, tears, full whisper).
